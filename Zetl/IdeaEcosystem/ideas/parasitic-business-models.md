# Parasitic Business Models

Type: Idea (Parasite)

## Overview
Ideas that exploit other ideas without contributing value, extracting resources while weakening their hosts.

## Core Principle
Parasitic ideas thrive by leveraging existing ecosystems, extracting value from host ideas while providing minimal benefit in return, often leading to the decline of their hosts.
