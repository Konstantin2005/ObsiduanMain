# Competitive Advantage

Type: Idea (Predator)

## Overview
The strategic positioning that allows an idea or organization to outperform competitors.

## Core Principle
Competitive advantage creates sustainable differentiation that makes it difficult for others to replicate or surpass the leading idea.
