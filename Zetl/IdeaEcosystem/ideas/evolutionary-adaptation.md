# Evolutionary Adaptation

Type: Idea (Symbiont)

## Overview
The process by which ideas evolve and adapt to changing environments, becoming more resilient and effective over time.

## Core Principle
Evolutionary adaptation allows ideas to survive by continuously improving their fit with their environment, learning from failures, and incorporating successful innovations.
