# Structured_Decision_Making

## Overview
A systematic approach to decision-making that incorporates evidence evaluation, alternative analysis, and bias mitigation.

## Detailed Content
Structured decision-making provides a framework for making better decisions by systematically analyzing alternatives, evaluating evidence, and mitigating cognitive biases throughout the decision process.

### Implementation Steps
1. **Define the problem**: Clearly articulate the decision needed
2. **Gather information**: Collect relevant data and evidence
3. **Generate alternatives**: Brainstorm multiple possible solutions
4. **Evaluate options**: Analyze pros and cons systematically
5. **Make the decision**: Choose based on objective criteria
6. **Monitor outcomes**: Track results and learn from experience

### Required Skills
- Analytical thinking
- Evidence evaluation
- Alternative generation
- Critical analysis
- Outcome monitoring

### Practice Exercises
- Use decision matrices
- Conduct pre-mortem analysis
- Practice scenario planning
- Seek external validation
- Implement feedback loops
