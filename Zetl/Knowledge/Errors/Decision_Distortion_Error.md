# Decision_Distortion_Error

## Overview
The error of making poor decisions due to cognitive biases and thinking errors, leading to suboptimal outcomes.

## Detailed Content
Decision distortion error occurs when cognitive biases and thinking errors systematically distort the decision-making process, leading to choices that are suboptimal or harmful.

### Error Patterns
- **Biased information processing**: Distorting evidence to fit existing beliefs
- **Inadequate analysis**: Failing to consider all alternatives
- **Emotional influence**: Allowing emotions to override rational analysis

### Common Contexts
- Business strategy
- Personal relationships
- Career decisions
- Financial planning

### Detection Methods
- Conduct pre-mortem analysis
- Seek external perspectives
- Evaluate alternatives systematically
- Monitor decision outcomes
