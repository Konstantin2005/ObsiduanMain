# Status Quo Bias

## Overview
The preference for things to stay the same and resistance to change, even when change might be beneficial.

## Detailed Content
Status quo bias is the tendency to prefer the current state of affairs over alternative options, often resulting in inertia and resistance to beneficial change.

### Mechanisms
- **Change aversion**: Psychological discomfort with uncertainty
- **Loss focus**: Overemphasis on potential losses from change
- **Default effect**: Preference for existing options

### Real-World Impact
- Policy resistance
- Technology adoption
- Personal development
- Organizational change

### Psychological Basis
The bias combines loss aversion with cognitive ease, making the known (even if suboptimal) more appealing than the unknown (even if potentially better).
