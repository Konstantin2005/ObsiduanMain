# Health_Wellness

## Definition
Maintaining physical and mental well-being through balanced lifestyle choices and self-care practices.

## Importance
Health is the foundation for pursuing all other goals and values. Without good health, we cannot effectively contribute to personal growth, career development, or relationships.
