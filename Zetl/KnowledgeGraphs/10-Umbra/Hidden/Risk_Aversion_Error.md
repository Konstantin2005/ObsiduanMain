# Risk Aversion Error

## Overview
The error of avoiding risks even when the potential benefits outweigh the potential costs, leading to missed opportunities.

## Detailed Content
Risk aversion error occurs when individuals avoid taking calculated risks that could lead to significant gains, often due to an exaggerated fear of potential losses.

### Error Patterns
- **Excessive caution**: Avoiding all risks regardless of potential rewards
- **Opportunity cost neglect**: Focusing on potential losses rather than benefits
- **Loss focus**: Overweighting negative outcomes

### Common Contexts
- Career advancement
- Investment decisions
- Innovation adoption
- Personal growth

### Detection Methods
- Compare risk-return profiles
- Evaluate opportunity costs
- Consider long-term benefits
- Assess risk tolerance alignment
