# Resource_Waste_Error

## Overview
The error of misallocating resources (time, money, effort) due to cognitive biases and poor decision-making.

## Detailed Content
Resource waste error occurs when cognitive biases and thinking errors lead to inefficient allocation of resources, resulting in lost opportunities and reduced effectiveness.

### Error Patterns
- **Poor investment choices**: Allocating resources to low-return activities
- **Escalation of commitment**: Continuing investment in failing projects
- **Opportunity cost neglect**: Focusing on sunk costs rather than alternatives

### Common Contexts
- Business investments
- Project management
- Personal development
- Organizational resources

### Detection Methods
- Conduct cost-benefit analysis
- Monitor resource utilization
- Evaluate opportunity costs
- Track project outcomes
