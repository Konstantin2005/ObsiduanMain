# Escalation of Commitment Error

## Overview
The error of continuing to invest resources in a failing course of action due to previous investment, rather than cutting losses and changing strategy.

## Detailed Content
Escalation of commitment error occurs when individuals or organizations increase their investment in a failing course of action to justify previous investments, even when evidence suggests the course is wrong.

### Error Patterns
- **Increasing investment**: Adding more resources to failing projects
- **Rationalization**: Justifying continued investment with optimistic projections
- **Denial of failure**: Ignoring or minimizing negative evidence

### Common Contexts
- Business project management
- Financial investments
- Sports coaching decisions
- Military strategy

### Detection Methods
- Conduct regular project reviews
- Set predetermined exit criteria
- Seek external perspectives
- Analyze opportunity costs
