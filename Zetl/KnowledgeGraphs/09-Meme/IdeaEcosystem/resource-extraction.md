# Resource Extraction

Type: Concept

## Overview
The process of taking resources from one system to benefit another, often without fair compensation or sustainable practices.

## Core Principle
Resource extraction prioritizes short-term gain over long-term sustainability, often depleting the host system's capacity to regenerate.
