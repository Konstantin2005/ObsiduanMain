# Mutual Aid Systems

Type: Counteridea

## Overview
The principle that communities thrive by sharing resources and supporting each other's success rather than competing.

## Core Principle
Mutual aid challenges competitive paradigms by demonstrating that collective prosperity creates more value than individual accumulation.
