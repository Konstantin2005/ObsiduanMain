# Predatory Technology

Type: Idea (Predator)

## Overview
Ideas that dominate and destroy competing ideas, establishing market leadership through superior capabilities and strategic positioning.

## Core Principle
Predatory ideas eliminate weaker competitors, consolidate market share, and create barriers to entry that prevent new ideas from challenging their dominance.
