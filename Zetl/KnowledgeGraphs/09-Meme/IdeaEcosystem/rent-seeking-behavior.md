# Rent-Seeking Behavior

Type: Idea (Parasite)

## Overview
The practice of obtaining economic rent by manipulating the economic environment rather than creating new value.

## Core Principle
Rent-seeking extracts value from existing systems without contributing to productivity or innovation, often through regulation or market manipulation.
