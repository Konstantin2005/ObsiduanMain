# Financial_Stress_Error

## Overview
The error of experiencing financial stress due to poor financial decisions caused by cognitive biases and thinking errors.

## Detailed Content
Financial stress error occurs when cognitive biases and thinking errors lead to poor financial decisions, resulting in financial difficulties and stress.

### Error Patterns
- **Impulsive spending**: Making purchases without considering long-term impact
- **Overconfidence in investments**: Taking excessive risks
- **Loss aversion**: Holding losing investments too long
- **Status comparison**: Spending to keep up with others

### Common Contexts
- Investment decisions
- Consumer spending
- Savings planning
- Debt management

### Detection Methods
- Track spending patterns
- Monitor investment performance
- Compare with financial goals
- Seek financial advice
