# Echo_Chamber_Error

## Overview
The error of creating self-reinforcing environments where only similar viewpoints are heard, leading to distorted thinking and poor decisions.

## Detailed Content
Echo chamber error occurs when individuals or groups surround themselves with like-minded people and information sources, creating environments that reinforce existing beliefs and limit exposure to diverse perspectives.

### Error Patterns
- **Selective exposure**: Seeking only confirming information
- **Group polarization**: Intensifying existing beliefs through group discussion
- **Information filtering**: Excluding dissenting viewpoints

### Common Contexts
- Social media algorithms
- Professional networks
- Academic disciplines
- Political communities

### Detection Methods
- Seek diverse sources
- Challenge group consensus
- Monitor information diversity
- Practice viewpoint diversity
