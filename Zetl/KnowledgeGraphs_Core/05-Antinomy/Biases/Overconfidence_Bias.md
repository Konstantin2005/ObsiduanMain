# Overconfidence Bias

## Overview
The tendency for people to have excessive confidence in their own abilities, knowledge, or judgments.

## Detailed Content
Overconfidence bias is a cognitive bias where individuals overestimate their knowledge, abilities, or the accuracy of their predictions. This bias affects decision-making across personal, professional, and academic domains.

### Mechanisms
- **Precision illusion**: Believing one knows more than one actually does
- **Outcome bias**: Judging decisions based on outcomes rather than process
- **Hindsight bias**: Believing, after an event has occurred, that one would have predicted it

### Real-World Impact
- Financial trading losses
- Medical diagnosis errors
- Project management failures
- Investment decisions

### Psychological Basis
Overconfidence stems from the brain's tendency to create coherent narratives and fill gaps in knowledge with plausible but potentially incorrect information.
