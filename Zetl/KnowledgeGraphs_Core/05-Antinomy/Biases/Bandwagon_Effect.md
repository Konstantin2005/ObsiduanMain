# Bandwagon Effect

## Overview
The tendency to adopt beliefs or behaviors because many other people do, often without independent evaluation.

## Detailed Content
The bandwagon effect is a cognitive bias where people adopt certain beliefs or behaviors simply because others are doing so, often without critical evaluation or independent thinking.

### Mechanisms
- **Social conformity**: Pressure to align with group norms
- **Information cascade**: Following others' actions as information
- **Reputation concerns**: Fear of social exclusion

### Real-World Impact
- Market trends
- Political opinions
- Technology adoption
- Peer pressure

### Psychological Basis
The bandwagon effect combines social psychology with cognitive biases, creating powerful pressures to conform and follow group behavior.
