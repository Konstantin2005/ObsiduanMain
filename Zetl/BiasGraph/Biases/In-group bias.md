# In-group bias

- [[Non sequitur]]
- [[Nutshell fallacy]]

## Усиливает
- [[Loss aversion]]
- [[Mere exposure effect]]

## Ослабляется
- [[Regret minimization]]
- [[Second-order effects analysis]]
