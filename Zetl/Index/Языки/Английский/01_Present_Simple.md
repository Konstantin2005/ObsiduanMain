# Present Simple  
  
## 📌 Use  
- habits  
- facts  
- routines  
  
---  
  
## 📌 Structure  
  
### Positive  
I/You/We/They + V1  
He/She/It + V1 + s/es  
  
Example:  
- I work  
- She works  
  
---  
  
## 📌 S / ES rules  
  
Add **-s**  
- work → works  
- play → plays  
  
Add **-es**  
- watch → watches  
- go → goes  
- fix → fixes  
  
---  
  
## 📌 -y rule  
Consonant + y → ies  
- study → studies  
  
Vowel + y → s  
- play → plays  
  
---  
  
## 📌 Negative  
do/does + not + V1  
- He does not work  
  
---  
  
## 📌 Questions  
Do/Does + subject + V1?  
- Does she work?
