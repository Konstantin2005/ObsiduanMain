## Feedback Loops

### Values Feedback
- Reflections link back to Values
- Projects link to Values
- Goals link to Values

### Continuous Improvement
- Decisions inform Projects
- Projects inform Goals
- Goals inform Values
- Reflections inform Decisions
- Decisions inform Reflections

## System Usage

1. **Start with Values**: Identify your core values to guide your system
2. **Set Goals**: Create specific goals aligned with your values
3. **Plan Projects**: Develop concrete projects to achieve your goals
4. **Make Decisions**: Make conscious choices about your actions
5. **Reflect**: Learn from your experiences and decisions
6. **Iterate**: Use insights to refine your values, goals, and plans
