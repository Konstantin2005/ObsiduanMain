# Career_Stagnation_Error

## Overview
The error of experiencing career stagnation due to poor career decisions caused by cognitive biases and thinking errors.

## Detailed Content
Career stagnation error occurs when cognitive biases and thinking errors lead to poor career decisions, resulting in limited growth and advancement opportunities.

### Error Patterns
- **Skill neglect**: Failing to develop new competencies
- **Risk aversion**: Avoiding challenging opportunities
- **Confirmation bias**: Ignoring career development feedback
- **Status quo bias**: Resisting necessary changes

### Common Contexts
- Job selection
- Skill development
- Career transitions
- Professional networking

### Detection Methods
- Track skill development
- Monitor career progress
- Seek feedback regularly
- Evaluate opportunities objectively
