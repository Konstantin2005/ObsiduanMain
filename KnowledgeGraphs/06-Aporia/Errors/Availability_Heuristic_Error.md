# Availability Heuristic Error

## Overview
The error of making decisions based on readily available examples rather than comprehensive statistical analysis or objective evidence.

## Detailed Content
The availability heuristic error occurs when individuals overestimate the importance of information that is readily available to them, often due to recency, vividness, or emotional impact. This leads to distorted judgments and suboptimal decisions.

### Error Patterns
- **Recency bias**: Overweighting recent events
- **Emotional amplification**: Giving disproportionate weight to emotional events
- **Media distortion**: Relying on news coverage for probability assessment

### Common Contexts
- Risk assessment in daily life
- Financial investment decisions
- Health and safety evaluations
- Professional judgment and expertise

### Detection Methods
- Compare intuitive judgments with statistical data
- Seek disconfirming evidence
- Consider base rates and long-term trends
