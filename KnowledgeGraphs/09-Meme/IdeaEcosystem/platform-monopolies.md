# Platform Monopolies

Type: Meme

## Overview
The cultural phenomenon where dominant digital platforms control entire ecosystems, limiting competition and innovation.

## Core Principle
Platform monopolies leverage network effects and data control to create insurmountable barriers, stifling alternative approaches and concentrating power.
